## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Core Philosophy
The design system reflects heritage, precision mechanical mastery, and generational reliability. Rooted in Huntington Beach, it bridges the exacting standards of European automotive engineering (BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Porsche, Audi, VW) with the welcoming, transparent service of an established family-owned neighborhood shop. 

The aesthetic is utilitarian, structural, and tactile rather than sterile or hyper-digital. It avoids decorative drop shadows and ethereal effects, relying instead on clean tectonic planes, crisp structural rules, bold contrast, and deliberate color blocking.

### Aesthetic Movement: Modern Industrial & Utilitarian Tectonic
- **High-contrast structural framing:** Thick borders, clean dividers (`1px` to `2px`), and clear containment establish clarity and mechanical rigor.
- **Deep Maritime Foundations:** Dominated by dark maritime navy (`#0f2340`) evoking legacy engineering, grounded on warm cream (`#fbf8f3`) surfaces that bring human warmth and approachability.
- **Urgent Mechanical Accents:** Automotive safety orange (`#ee7a1f`) is used purposefully for high-value actions, status indicators, and focus states.
- **WCAG Ergonomics First:** Built around generous tactile hit targets (minimum 44px to 48px), stark contrast ratios (well above 7:1 for text), and unapologetic 3px focus indicators.

## Colors

### Palette Architecture
The palette relies on deliberate tonal balance:
- **Primary Navy (`#0f2340`)**: Anchor for navigation, hero blocks, primary brand headers, and high-impact UI elements.
- **Secondary Automotive Orange (`#ee7a1f`)**: High-visibility safety accent. Reserved exclusively for primary action conversion (Estimate, Call Now), interactive focus rings, rating stars, and active process markers.
- **Tertiary Deep Navy (`#17335c`)**: Structural companion for image framing, strip badges, and completed pipeline stages.
- **Base Canvas (`#fbf8f3` Cream)**: Warm, non-glare body canvas preventing clinical harshness.
- **Neutral Muted Surface (`#eceff3` Gray)**: Cool, intermediate neutral for secondary feature grids and technical containers.

### Accessibility & Contrast Rules
- Dark navy (`#0f2340`) on white or cream achieves an AAA-compliant contrast ratio > 14:1.
- Body text (`#1a2433`) on cream (`#fbf8f3`) maintains an AAA rating of 12.8:1.
- Muted copy (`#556173`) ensures AA compliance > 5.2:1 against light canvases.
- High-visibility orange (`#ee7a1f`) is paired with white (`#ffffff`) or navy (`#0f2340`) for bold button typography and 3px keyboard outlines.

## Typography

### Structural Hierarchy
A single grotesque typeface (`Inter`) handles the entire typographic spectrum, using tight negative tracking (`-0.02em`) on massive display weights (`800`) to evoke heavy-gauge machinery, paired with relaxed line heights (`1.6`) on neutral body weights (`400`) for effortless technical legibility.

### Application Rules
- **Display & Headings:** Rendered exclusively in Extra Bold (`800`) or Bold (`700`) with tight line-heights (`1.1` to `1.2`).
- **Eyebrow / Overlines:** Always uppercase with `0.12em` letter-spacing, rendered in brand orange (`#ee7a1f`) to introduce sections.
- **Body & Technical Specs:** Set to `17px` base or `16px` secondary with generous `1.6` line heights to maximize readability during field inspections or emergency lookups.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Model
The layout is governed by a bounded, center-aligned 12-column fluid grid locked to a max width of `1120px` (`70rem`) with `22px` (`1.375rem`) outer canvas safe margins.

- **Desktop Standard Sections:** `84px` (`5.25rem`) vertical padding to establish confident editorial breathing room.
- **Mobile Compression (`<= 820px`):** Section padding compresses to `60px`, and hero sections to `40px`.
- **Viewport Safe-Area Accommodation:** Includes `calc(68px + env(safe-area-inset-top))` scroll offsets and `body { padding-bottom: calc(72px + env(safe-area-inset-bottom)); }` on mobile to make room for fixed call bars.

### Grid Reflow Strategy
- **Hero & Primary Sections:** Dual-column asymmetric splits (`1fr 1.15fr`, `5fr 7fr`, `1.1fr 0.9fr`) with `56px` (`3.5rem`) gaps collapse into a stacked single-column (`1fr`) with `36px` vertical gaps on tablet/mobile screens (`<= 820px`).
- **Estimate Form Engine:** Dual input grids (`1fr 1fr`) seamlessly collapse into single-column vertical touch flows on mobile.

## Elevation & Depth

### Flat Tectonic Layering
This design system intentionally rejects diffuse drop shadows and soft blurred neomorphism. Depth is established through hard material surface stepping, color shifts, and high-contrast structural borders:

1. **Ground Canvas (Level 0):** Warm Canvas (`#fbf8f3`) or Ground Midnight (`#0a1a31`).
2. **Elevated Structural Surface (Level 1):** Cool Gray (`#eceff3`) panels or pure white (`#ffffff`) cards defined by a crisp `1px solid #dde2e8` border.
3. **Floating Overlay (Level 2):** Semi-opaque dark capsules (`rgba(15, 35, 64, 0.8)`) with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)` for photo badges.
4. **Interactive Focus / Highlights:** Sharp 3px automotive safety orange (`#ee7a1f`) outline rings with a `2px` or `3px` offset.
5. **Fixed Viewport Rails (Level 3):**
   - Sticky Header (`z-index: 10`): Navy surface anchored to top.
   - Fixed Mobile Action Bar (`z-index: 15`): Navy pinned bottom bar with `1px solid #243a5a` border-top.

## Shapes

### Geometric Discipline
The shape language combines functional industrial rounded rectangles with purpose-built circular controls:

- **Base Interactive Controls (`8px`):** Buttons, text inputs, textareas, selects, and alert banners use `0.5rem` (`8px`) radii for a solid, mechanical feel.
- **Pill Selectables (`10px`):** Custom checkbox and radio cards use `10px` radii to indicate touch targets.
- **Structural Component Cards (`14px` - `16px`):** Review cards, business hours panels, media wrappers, and estimate wizard containers use `14px` to `16px` radii bounded by crisp borders.
- **Circular Utilities (`50%`):** Carousel previous/next navigation discs and media controls use perfect 50% circular geometries (`46px x 46px` and `40px x 40px`).

## Components

### 1. Buttons & Triggers
- **Primary CTA (`.btn`):**
  - Background: `#ee7a1f` (Safety Orange), Color: `#0f2340` (Navy) or `#ffffff`.
  - Border: `2px solid #ee7a1f`.
  - Geometry: Height `48px`, Padding `0 24px`, Radius `8px`, Font: `16px / 700`.
  - Hover: `filter: brightness(1.07);`.
- **Secondary Ghost / Outline Light (`.btn.o`):**
  - Background: `transparent`, Color: `#ffffff`.
  - Border: `2px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.55)`.
- **Dark Outline (`.btn.d`):**
  - Background: `transparent`, Color: `#0f2340`.
  - Border: `2px solid #0f2340`.
- **Interactive Focus:** `outline: 3px solid #ee7a1f; outline-offset: 3px;`.

### 2. Form Controls & Multi-Step Wizard
- **Text Inputs, Selects & Textareas:**
  - Height: `48px` (inputs/selects), Padding: `10px 14px`, Radius: `8px`.
  - Border: `1.5px solid #8f9bab`, Background: `#ffffff`, Text: `#1a2433`.
  - Focus: `outline: 3px solid #ee7a1f; outline-offset: 2px; border-color: #ee7a1f;`.
  - Error: `border-color: #b3261e;` accompanied by `14px` `#b3261e` error text below.
- **Service Selection Cards (Checkbox / Radio):**
  - Padding: `12px 16px`, Radius: `10px`, Border: `1.5px solid #dde2e8`, Background: `#ffffff`.
  - Selected State: Background shifts to `#eef3fa` with Border `1.5px solid #0f2340`.
- **Progress Stepper Bar (`.prog`):**
  - List items with a prominent `4px solid #dde2e8` top rule.
  - Active Step: Top rule becomes `4px solid #ee7a1f` with bold `#0f2340` label.
  - Completed Step: Top rule becomes `4px solid #17335c`.

### 3. Cards & Review Quotes
- **Customer Review Cards (`.rc`):**
  - Background: `#ffffff`, Border: `1px solid #dde2e8`, Radius: `14px`, Padding: `26px`.
  - Header: Star rating group with 5 SVGs filled with `#ee7a1f` (`20px x 20px`, gap `2px`).
  - Quote Typography: `16px / 1.6` text clamped cleanly at 7 lines.
  - Footer Divider: Top hairline `1px solid #dde2e8` separating verified badge and reviewer location metadata.
- **Mobile Review Carousel Behavior:**
  - Desktop uses a 3-column grid (`gap: 20px`).
  - Screen sizes `<= 820px` switch to an accessible horizontal scroll snap container (`scroll-snap-type: x mandatory; flex: 0 0 86%;`).

### 4. Automotive Marquee & Service Badges
- **Infinite Brand Carousel:**
  - Mask: Linear edge gradient fade `linear-gradient(90deg, transparent, #000 7%, #000 93%, transparent)`.
  - Includes a circular accessible Pause/Play toggle (`40px x 40px`, border `1.5px solid #0f2340`) that pauses keyframe `@keyframes lm` and respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Mechanical Icon Containers:**
  - Primary Service Box: `48px x 48px` container, radius `12px`, background `#0f2340`, housing a `26px x 26px` inline SVG with `#ffffff` stroke (`stroke-width: 1.8`).
  - Secondary Service Box: `40px x 40px` container, radius `10px`, background `#17335c`, housing a `22px x 22px` white stroke SVG.

### 5. Mobile Fixed Conversion Bar (`.cb`)
- Persistent bottom action bar pinned at viewport bottom (`z-index: 15`).
- Background: `#0f2340`, Border-top: `1px solid #243a5a`.
- Height: `72px` with integrated `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`.
- Contains immediate dial trigger with prominent phone icon and orange call-to-action button.
